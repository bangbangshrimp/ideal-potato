About Me

Hello, my name is Ruhin Chakravarty. I am a sophomore at Drexel University majoring in Computer Science. I have chosen to take this CS 180 course because I want to be able to get a head start on my education in AI, since I want to do something with AI in the future for my career. I feel like AI is the future for a majority of people in the future, and companies will have to start adapting to AI to be able to keep up with this future. One time AI did show up in my life was when I was trying to organize my study schedule for my finals. AI was able to help me to create an efficient study schedule for all my final exams, which led to more success during my finals than would have happened had I not been effective with my studying.

One honest concern I have about AI has to do with the recent headlines about how AI might be getting out of control and could end humanity in a few decades. While I don’t think this is entirely true, I feel like employees coming out and saying things like this about AI should bring up conversations about how to properly control and use AI to humanity’s benefit, and make sure we use it as a tool for success for the human race. The course objective I’m most curious about is “Describe how core AI techniques such as search and adversarial reasoning are used to model intelligent behavior.” This interests me because I have always wondered how AI was able to develop to the point that it is at right now, since it seems like something magical. Something about me is that I am a big Philly sports fan. I love all the Philly area teams and I’m always excited to cheer them on.
